DEMO-LIES Project

Detecting Erosion of Democratic Norms via Language Analysis

Project Overview

The DEMO-LIES project investigates subtle forms of democratic erosion by analyzing political discourse. Using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP), we aim to detect partisan bias, misinformation, and rhetoric that undermine democratic institutions.

My contribution involves developing NLP models to identify these linguistic markers in large corpora of political texts, speeches, and media articles.

  • Advanced NLP Techniques
  • Bias Detection in Media
  • Tech Stack: TensorFlow, Pandas, Scikit-learn

Key Research Areas

Partisan Bias Detection

Using classifier entropy and fine-tuned BERT models to measure uncertainty and bias in political news articles.

Framing Analysis

Investigating how different political actors frame key issues to influence public opinion and policy debates.

Democratic Norm Erosion

Tracking the use of rhetoric that challenges democratic norms, such as attacking the legitimacy of opponents or institutions.
